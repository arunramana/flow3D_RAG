FLOW-3D HYDRO Workshops

Our online and local workshops introduce FLOW-3D HYDRO software to civil and environmental engineers through a series of guided, hands-on exercises.

  • Our online workshops begin with a live presentation and overview of CFD fundamentals, and progress with weekly video tutorials and exercises designed to develop your understanding and skills in CFD modeling.
  • Our local workshops offer an in-person experience while providing a thorough introduction to CFD for free surface hydraulics.
  • Workshop attendees receive a 30-day FLOW-3D HYDRO license.

By attending one of our workshops, you will learn the FLOW-3D HYDRO user interface and the steps of setting up, running and analyzing simulations, become familiar with CFD modeling best practices, and will have the opportunity to explore advanced modeling topics relevant to your application.

If you are a current FLOW-3D HYDRO user interested in training, please visit our Training page for in-person training options.

FAQs

Who should attend?

  • Our FLOW-3D HYDRO Workshops are designed for professional engineers and engineers-in-training who work in water-related infrastructure, including hydropower, flood control, port and coastal structures, bridges and culverts, dam infrastructure, and environmental restoration.

What will you learn?

  • How to set up, run and post-process free surface hydraulic models.
  • How to add complexity by including additional physics such sediment transport, scour, particles or aeration.
  • How to use sophisticated visualization tools to effectively analyze and convey simulation results.

You’ve completed the workshop, what’s next?

Upon completion of the workshop, we will invite you to meet with a Flow Science engineer to discuss how we can help your organization bring FLOW-3D HYDRO modeling into your water infrastructure projects. 

Technical Requirements
  • A Windows machine running 64 bit Windows 10 or 11
  • Laptops are provided at our training classroom in Chapel Hill
  • An external mouse (not a touchpad device)
  • Dual monitor setup recommended
  • Dedicated graphics card; nVidia Quadro card required for remote desktop
For more info on recommended hardware, see our Supported Platforms page.
Workshop Registration, Cancellation and Licensing Policy

Registration: Workshop registration is available to prospective users in the US and Canada. Prospective users outside of these countries should contact their distributor to inquire about workshops.  If you are a current FLOW-3D HYDRO user interested in training, please visit our Training page for customized online and in-person training options.

Cancellation: Flow Science reserves the right to cancel a workshop at any time, due to reasons such as insufficient registrations or instructor unavailability. In such cases, a full refund will be given, or attendees may opt to transfer their registration to another workshop. Flow Science is not responsible for any costs incurred.

Registrants who are unable to attend a workshop may cancel up to one week in advance to receive a full refund. Attendees must cancel their registration by 5pm MT (7pm ET) one week prior to the date of the workshop; after that date, no refunds will be given. If available, an attendee can also request to have their registration transferred to another workshop.

Licensing: Workshop licenses are for evaluation purposes only, and not to be used for any commercial purpose other than evaluation of the capabilities of the software.
